**NLL/scale treatment is correct.** The target derives `log(sigma)+(y-mu)^2/(2 sigma^2)` with its Normal constant; derivatives in mean, log-SD and the actual softplus raw coordinate agree by chain rule. **Uncertainty and regularization boundaries are sound.** At fixed fitted weights the interval concerns observation noise, with no claim that parameter uncertainty vanishes in reality. The scope correctly restricts equivalence with decoupled shrinkage and excludes general adaptive-optimizer equivalence, sparse-prior surveys and Bayesian weight fitting.
