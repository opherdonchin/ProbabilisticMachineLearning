## Coordinator's account of the exchange

The old process had backpressure rules but operated as serial contracts and verdicts. Organization supplied a fixed pair; Design specified a full explanation; Authoring reported overflow; the coordinator commissioned another Design repair under the same count/title restrictions. That did not give the owners an active opportunity to negotiate which restrictions were necessary or to compare feasible alternatives before freezing them.

Organization traced the actual authority: the instructor accepted the derivation live. Two slides and their titles were coordinator/stage choices; 2–4 minutes was an unmeasured estimate. The coordinator's later task envelope made count/titles binding for a repair without making them instructor decisions. Those choices can be proposed for revision by their owners. Mathematical depth, live placement, section endpoint and explicit instructor constraints remain protected. A provisional owner revision is not instructor approval.

Design offered a compact context/averaging region above supporting moment calculations beside the result/optimum region. It distinguished necessary dependencies from negotiable duplication, headings, persistence, reveal count and placement. Authoring initially offered a spanning result row, then recognized that it largely repeated the failed stack and counteroffered a genuinely adjacent realization. Organization accepted that semantic arrangement as a provisional alternative if the evaluated terms visibly support the identity and optimality follows it.

The participants also offered three-slide divisions. They did not agree to adopt one: an identity at the second frame's endpoint differs from reserving it for the third. That difference belongs to Organization and needs evidence about the reasoning and time, not an automatic count increase. The existing proof depth and actual lecture remain unchanged.

The agreed next evidence, if slide work is commissioned, is a bounded comparison of genuinely different provisional arrangements at the same course scale: the difficult complete state and first averaging state, followed by all-state examination of a viable candidate. The experiment must test readable dependency relationships as well as fit and grouping. It cannot establish classroom effectiveness or instructor acceptance. No such comparison was run during this process discussion.

## Operational change

Keep the relevant two or three specialists active around one bounded cluster. Each offers what it can preserve, change and test; the affected owners respond with conditions, counteroffers or a useful rejection. The coordinator circulates the alternatives, distinguishes authority from assumptions, and chooses the next evidence-producing step. Each specialist adopts changes only in its own artifact.

Scope and Section Design retain their intellectual contracts and ordinary lightweight checks. Organization, Design and Authoring converge on a coherent set before those slide-level decisions freeze. Explicitly scoped authoring prototypes may inform provisional design; they are exploratory builds, not read-only feasibility reviews or authorized production replacements. Existing instructor approval gates remain. Convergence requires both a realizable output and a readable teaching argument; agreement among agents is insufficient by itself. If alternatives expose a consequential teaching tradeoff, bring the concrete choice to the instructor.
