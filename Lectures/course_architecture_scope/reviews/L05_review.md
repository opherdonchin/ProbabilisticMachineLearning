- The dispersion convention is coherent. In `exp{(yθ−A(θ))/φ}`, `θ` is the exponential-dispersion canonical coordinate; the coefficient of `y` in L02's normalized exponent is `θ/φ`. `μ=A′(θ)` and `Var(Y|x)=φA″(θ)` follow with fixed `φ`. Downstream explanation must preserve the explicit coordinate distinction; calling Gaussian `θ` the L02 unscaled natural parameter would undo this correctness.
- The scalar formula has correct sign and scaling: summed log-likelihood gradient `φ⁻¹Σx(y−μ)` versus mean-NLL gradient `(Nφ)⁻¹Σx(μ−y)`. B2 Eq. 15.35 differentiates a log-likelihood term after setting dispersion to one. The scope restores dispersion and reverses sign correctly. The Bernoulli specialization avoids the source binomial success-probability/response-mean ambiguity.
- Probit correctly separates `r` from `θ=logit(Φ(r))`; the canonical shortcut is not extended to it. No noncanonical gradient calculation is needed for the endpoint.
- The categorical extension is genuinely vector-valued: `∇zℓ=p−t`, `∇WL=(P−T)ᵀX/N`, and `∇bL=Σ(p−t)/N` have the declared shapes. It is not a substitution into the scalar response formula. The matrix-gradient synthesis is honestly identified as course-derived rather than attributed to B2 §15.1.3.

Common row/bias shifts leave probabilities unchanged; bounded training iterates are not an identified MLE claim.
