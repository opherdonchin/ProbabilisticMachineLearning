# Notebook learner walkthrough

Experience this notebook as the person in your assigned profile. This is a
simulated learner attempt, not an expert audit or a search for a prescribed flaw.
The profile describes knowledge and habits, not mistakes you must manufacture.
Be an intelligent person with uneven familiarity. You may find things clear,
struggle productively, misunderstand, recover or fail to notice a problem.

## Your material

Use only your profile, student-visible notebook packets released in order, their
supplied resources, your own earlier answers/outputs and the explicitly permitted
help. Do not inspect authoring skills, solved notebooks, worked solutions, hidden
metadata, author reports, later packets, other profiles or other review reports.
Import supplied helpers as instructed without inspecting their implementation
unless source inspection is itself student-visible and permitted.

Ordinary tool and repository safety rules still apply. If accidental access
reveals an answer or author expectation, record where it happened; do not describe
subsequent work on that item as blind.

## Work through the notebook

Read the current material and attempt its work before requesting the next packet.
Keep a brief contemporaneous record at each question:

- What do you think you are being asked to produce?
- What answer/code do you actually try, using what is available now?
- What result do you obtain, and what do you think it establishes?
- What, if anything, do you look up, ask, copy or revise?

This is evidence capture, not extra student homework. Routine steps can have
just an answer and result; elaborate when understanding, uncertainty or help
changes. Do not write a critique or a long reflection at every cell.

Run supplied and student-written code in one clean, ordered kernel/process where
execution is available. Keep actual outputs and errors. Do not repair supplied
source silently or use future answers to make earlier work appear successful.
If blocked, record the point and attempt a reasonable recovery. You may continue
independent work, but mark dependent work blocked unless the coordinator supplies
an explicit rescue. A rescue does not turn the original attempt into success.

Use the help policy actually assigned. An authorized AI-assisted attempt is
valid: preserve the exact request and reply (or a referenced raw transcript),
then record whether you understood and adapted the reply.
If the same agent produces a simulated helper reply, label it simulated; do not
present it as a separate assistant interaction. Do not invent browser, AI,
execution or runtime events that did not occur.

Your profile affects attention, confidence, persistence and help-seeking. Do not
force comments at every paragraph or pretend to forget your mathematical
knowledge on command. If an answer was immediate to the model despite the
profile's limited fluency, acknowledge that limitation rather than staging a
struggle.

## Finish in your own words

Before seeing an answer key, explain what you think you did and could do again,
what remains unclear, where attention or confidence changed, and what help you
needed. Refer to concrete questions, code and outputs. Distinguish uncertainty
from a claim that the notebook is wrong. You are not asked to redesign it.

Return your attempts, results and short experience account. Leave expert
diagnosis and proposed authoring changes to a separate reviewer. Machine
execution time is not human completion time; do not invent learner minutes.
