## Recommended intellectual division

The sequence develops two complementary answers to **how a learned representation should use the structure of its input**. L10 develops local composition and parameter sharing for spatial data. L11 develops contextual representations and information routing for ordered data. Each has one principal application and notebook. Probabilistic outputs, likelihood fitting and inference-network roles connect them to the existing course; they are not a separate deep-learning survey.

### L10 contract: representation learning with structural constraints

**Starting boundary:** Gaussian/categorical models and likelihoods; L05 logits, cross-entropy, reverse-mode differentiation, minibatch updates and first PyTorch encounter; L03's distinction between a probability graph and a numerical program. L06–L09 supply additional inference context, not required new CNN mathematics.

Develop the learned-feature progression

$$
p_\theta(y\mid x)=\operatorname{Cat}\{y;\operatorname{softmax}(W h_\phi(x)+b)\},\qquad
h^{(l+1)}=\varphi_l(W_l h^{(l)}+b_l).
$$

**Derive/work** affine collapse without nonlinearities; work a small composed feature calculation. Then **derive/work** convolution as a sparse matrix with tied entries:

$$
a_{i,j,d}=b_d+\sum_{u,v,c}K_{u,v,c,d}h_{i+u,j+v,c}.
$$

A `k×k` convolution with `C_in,C_out` channels has `k² C_in C_out+C_out` parameters independent of image area, whereas an unrestricted dense map between flattened spatial fields generally does not. Explain precisely what locality and weight sharing assume and what they can sacrifice. Fewer parameters alone guarantees neither better generalization nor cheaper computation for every implementation. **Prove a bounded shift-equivariance identity** for the declared lattice/boundary regime; distinguish it from a classifier's invariance. **Derive/work** receptive-field growth for repeated stride-one local layers, then interpret part-to-part composition. This supplies an actual reason to discuss depth, without promising a universal superiority theorem.

**Apply** L05 reverse accumulation through composition and shared parameters. **Derive a short local identity** `J_(x+F(x))=I+J_F(x)` to explain residual paths, without claiming they prevent all vanishing/exploding gradients. **State/explain** sensible initialization, validation stopping and the role of normalization; do not rederive the training library or survey regularizers. More flexible function learning loses the affine canonical-GLM convexity guarantee.

**State/apply** that a numeric network can output parameters of different selected distributions: categorical logits here; a Gaussian mean and positive scale as a short connection. Network architecture and observation likelihood are different choices. Do not retain the former L11's complete mean/scale derivative, interval-analysis and prior-shrinkage modules as additional live obligations. The existing heteroskedastic example remains reusable evidence, not a required second notebook. Fixed-weight probabilities are not a posterior over weights.

**Extent envelope:** target 100 substantive +12 reserve minutes. A plausible allocation is 25 learned-feature/composition, 35 convolution/sharing/equivariance/receptive-field mathematics, 20 training/depth/residual reasoning, 20 probabilistic interpretation and application judgment. These are course-level allocations, not section boundaries. Estimated **tight**, not measured. Do not fund it by assuming CNNs are already familiar or by compressing the learned-representation argument into a definition.

### L11 contract: representing and retrieving sequence context

Return to a genuine task from L02: probability prediction for discrete sequences. The main notebook uses the existing source's **Time Machine** character corpus, so the continuity is reuse of the Markov-learning method, not reuse of the names dataset. Learn from a fixed training portion and evaluate per-token log score on a separate text portion. Generated-looking text is not the performance criterion.

**Derive/work** the autoregressive likelihood and reuse the categorical output:

$$
p_\theta(y_{1:T})=\prod_t p_\theta(y_t\mid y_{<t}),\qquad
h_t=f_\theta(h_{t-1},E y_{t-1}),\qquad
p_\theta(y_t\mid y_{<t})=\operatorname{Cat}(y_t;\operatorname{softmax}(Uh_t+b)).
$$

The recurrent state is a deterministic function of the available prefix, not an HMM latent state requiring marginalization. A first-order Markov assumption restricts dependence to the last observed token; a fixed-dimensional recurrent summary can express longer dependence but does not guarantee retention or learnability of arbitrarily distant information. **Work** shared recurrent parameters and a short unrolled derivative, and **explain** products of Jacobians as a source of long-range training difficulty. Teacher-forced likelihood conditions on the observed prefix; generation feeds back the model's draws. These are different operations with different failure modes.

**Derive/work** a small attention lookup and its matrix realization:

$$
A=\operatorname{rowsoftmax}(QK^T/\sqrt{d_k}+M),\qquad H=AV,
$$

where the causal mask M excludes unavailable future positions. Trace a concrete query, keys, weights and retrieved value; distinguish input-dependent retrieval weights from fixed learned projection weights. **Work/explain** the need to represent order and the consequence of allowing future information. Unpositioned, unmasked self-attention is permutation equivariant, not a general order-sensitive sequence model. **State/explain** multihead/block composition, positional information, residual paths and normalization as the transformer construction; no second optimizer or full transformer derivation is required.

**Compare** dense full-sequence attention's `O(T²d+Td²)` computation and quadratic attention storage with ordinary recurrent `O(Td²)` work at fixed widths and sequential state dependency. These are declared-regime orders, not blanket latency rankings; distinguish parallel training from autoregressive generation/caching. Use the comparison to choose among local convolution, recurrent compression and direct context retrieval, not to rank them as successively better algorithms.

Finally **state/explain** the two probabilistic roles: a network can parameterize model conditionals, or map evidence to parameters of an approximation `q_phi(z|x)`. The second role may assist a non-neural model. L09 has already introduced the optimization-to-shared-map distinction; L11 now makes its compositional implementation intelligible. L12 owns its VAE construction and objective.

**Extent envelope:** 100 substantive +12 reserve minutes: 30 autoregressive/recurrent representation and likelihood, 20 unrolling/training/context limitations, 30 worked causal attention, 12 structured choice/block composition and 8 model-versus-inference synthesis. Estimated **tight**. Drop gating mechanisms and architecture history first; never substitute a list of names for the worked retrieval argument.
