## Local authoring backpressure and fidelity checks

During first lowering, Authoring identified that orientation side-by-side columns would shrink the exact m/S formulas. Design accepted vertical stacking with both labeled groups simultaneously visible. This is a deliberate geometry revision under R06/R08/R15, not omission of mathematical content. The exact design was updated before freeze.

Coordinator inspection of the first authoring draft caught omitted object titles that carried local operation/warrant information, and an integral-label implementation typo. These are **implementation defects**, not evidence that the rules were absent from the exact design. Restoration and mathematical correction were completed before the production baseline was frozen. A further Design fidelity check noticed that the full joint density on slide3 was delayed to state2 while the design requires it in state1; the author was notified. The final rendered sequence verifies these repairs.

The coordinator's first full render inspection also caught a new reveal-helper markup/code boundary defect: literal Typst function-call text appeared on slides although compilation completed. Authoring corrected it before final visual assessment. This is **implementation/rendering failure**, not a mathematical design decision. It demonstrates why the ledger's production column cannot be completed from source and compile checks alone. The first malformed render is not the candidate baseline; the final corrected render was separately verified.

After the helper defects were corrected, actual geometry showed slide5 overflow9pt, slide6 overflow6pt and the combined quadratic slide overflow54pt. Design inspected those three completed states and negotiated: (a) place T and TΣ on one mathematical row; (b) replace duplicated row/transpose recall with the explicit connected product TΣTᵀ=(TΣ)Tᵀ, keeping the lower-left cancellation visible; (c) split inverse substitution and diagonal quadratic multiplication into two reasoning slides. The candidate therefore has17 slides/33 states, rather than16/32. No formula clearance was tightened to retain the initial count. This is **design–implementation capacity backpressure**, resolved by grouping and segmentation under R06/R08/R10/R15. Final actual-render revalidation was completed after the regrouping.
