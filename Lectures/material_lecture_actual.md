# Actual teaching and student notebook coverage

This record begins with the **first class meeting**. It is intentionally empty before the semester starts. Do not infer what was taught or completed from lecture decks, solved notebooks, approved scopes, or the [authored material ledger](material_ledger.md).

For each meeting, enter the actual date and the material substantively reached in the live lecture, any recordings made available, and the portions of the student notebook attempted or completed. Note changes from the [curriculum map](../curriculum_map.typ), topics postponed, observed time or difficulty, and the next meeting's necessary carry-forward. A mention or posted resource is not evidence of substantive student practice.

| Meeting and actual date | Lecture: taught/worked | Notebook: actually attempted | Deviations, omissions, follow-up | Evidence / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|

When actual delivery differs from the authored artifacts, keep the authored ledger unchanged as a record of preparation and enter the difference here. Revise the prospective curriculum map only when future teaching decisions change.
