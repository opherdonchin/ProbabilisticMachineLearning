## Why this is a justified pair, not merely a constructible topic allocation

Murphy B2 §16.1 defines a differentiable numerical computation and separates model conditionals from learned approximate-inference functions. Its progression then develops reusable operators and architectures. The revised scopes recover this generality without promising every operator: L10 develops learned composition and locality/sharing for spatial observations; L11 develops compression and retrieval for sequence context. Their mathematical arguments have value independently of the two notebook products.

L10 connects the existing affine categorical model to a learned feature map, explains why affine stacking collapses, and then derives the sparse/tied convolutional restriction. Shift equivariance, receptive-field growth, shared-gradient accumulation and held-out judgment explain what that restriction does and does not buy. This is a substantial continuation of the source's composition/architectural-structure argument. It avoids promoting the old Gaussian mean/scale example into the definition of neural modeling. A Gaussian output remains a useful short transfer showing likelihood and architecture are different choices.

L11 uses the existing sequence factorization to motivate a learned prefix representation. Its deterministic recurrent state is correctly distinguished from L08's random HMM state. Shared temporal derivatives, input-dependent attention retrieval, causal availability and order give mathematical substance to the architecture comparison. The inference-network ending then connects this numerical language to L09's objective and L12's local posterior. A package-backed RNN practical endpoint does not make attention a name-only live mention: a worked retrieval, masked matrix calculation and permutation argument are explicit live obligations.

The sacrificed material is named honestly: MH construction/balance, proposal design, formal mixing/finite-chain theory, HMC/NUTS mechanism derivations, and IS/AIS. These are not disguised later obligations. The new pair does not promise general architectural mastery, Bayesian weight uncertainty, verified feature semantics or universal performance advantage.

## Mathematical and primary-source checks

The core equations are consistent with the selected regimes. Convolution parameter storage is separated from spatial arithmetic; dense comparison requires matching declared shapes. The shift identity applies on the full lattice/periodic counterpart, with finite boundaries and subsampling qualified. The receptive-field formula applies only to the specified stride-one, equal-kernel, no-dilation stack. The residual Jacobian identity is not offered as a universal gradient-stability guarantee.
