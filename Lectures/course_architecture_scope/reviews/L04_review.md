## Findings, ownership and minimum treatments

### R1 — Conditional MI, DPI and sufficiency fit only at the promised limited depth

**Owner/severity:** Lecture Scope, **advisory**; exact time distribution later belongs to Section Design. **Evidence:** Sixteen minutes includes a new conditional-MI average-KL object, two chain decompositions, the CI-to-zero step, nonnegativity, a processing example and parameter-sufficiency equality. That chain is mathematically correct, but treating conditional MI as a second fully derived topic or adding a sufficient-statistic proof would overrun the allocation. The preceding 22-minute MI computation must already establish averaged conditional entropy; it cannot be assumed merely because students know conditioning.

**Provisional treatment:** Proceed with conditional MI and its chain rule as stated/explained proof support, DPI as a proof sketch, and only the constant/invertible binary processing extremes. **Minimum repair:** No required Scope change now. Protect these depth limits and explicitly return execution backpressure if a downstream attempt requires another conditional-MI calculation or proof. Shorten sufficiency to a statement before sacrificing the MI derivation or DPI argument. **Impact:** Downstream structure may redistribute time; no new model or source obligation is required. L12 receives MI/DPI tools, not representation optimization or a rate-distortion proof.

### R2 — The sufficiency recall needs a finite or clearly noncomputational parameter framing

**Owner/severity:** Lecture Scope, **advisory clarification**. **Evidence:** The spine says its treatment concerns finite/discrete objects and excludes differential entropy, but a random Bernoulli parameter often evokes a continuous Beta prior. No particular prior/data joint is fixed. The stated CI implication and MI equality are valid, and no numerical parameter MI is promised; the risk is that downstream explanation silently mixes discrete entropy formulas with a continuous parameter.

**Provisional treatment:** State `theta ⟂ D | S(D)` and its equality consequence without computing parameter entropy. **Minimum repair:** When making the recall concrete, either use a finite prior on a few candidate Bernoulli probabilities or explicitly keep the parameter example at CI/MI interpretation level with no discrete-entropy substitution. This can be settled during explanation design without changing coverage or requiring another derivation. **Impact:** Avoids a new differential-entropy/integral module. Counts preserve parameter-relevant information, not literal recovery of the original sequence; the scope already handles this source-language caveat correctly.

### R3 — Small state space does not by itself certify a 60-minute student workload

**Owner/severity:** Worked-solution/notebook authoring, **downstream-owned**. **Evidence:** The lab assigns axes orientation, likelihood/joint/marginal/posterior normalization, two MI calculations, ties, expected Bayes risk, a noise sweep and unequal-use comparison in one hour. There are only 128 pairs, so computation is small; the new skill burden is which axes to sum/max and how to avoid undefined expressions at zero mass. L01 practice does not establish independent broadcasting or masked-log expertise.

**Provisional treatment:** Accept the handoff because bit arrays, plotting, sweep harness and broadcasting are explicitly scaffolded. **Minimum repair:** Supply or isolate array-axis and zero-safe log/entropy infrastructure. Let students own the compact probability reductions and compare independently computed information quantities. Work the interior `alpha=0.2` case before interpreting supplied boundary behavior; do not spend the core hour debugging warnings or constructing plotting code. Keep simulation outside the core hour. **Impact:** Later validated worked solution must demonstrate normalization, support-aware posterior handling, information equality and timing with the actual student tasks. If the hour still fails with helpers, any endpoint reduction returns to Scope.

### R4 — Preserve distinct resource, information and decision quantities in the comparison

**Owner/severity:** Section Design/Slide Design/notebook explanation, **downstream-owned**. **Evidence:** At noise 0.2 the code improves message MI and block error using five rather than two channel uses, while MI per channel use decreases. Message entropy, codeword entropy, representation length and coding rate are distinct. The scope reports each correctly; collapsing them into a single unqualified improvement claim would undo the central interpretation. Uniform-prior joint MAP also minimizes message-block 0–1 loss, not every possible bitwise loss.

**Provisional treatment:** Retain the exact paired labels and block-level loss. **Minimum repair:** No Scope mathematical repair. Carry the channel-use count and information/error units into every live/lab comparison and avoid a capacity or efficiency claim. For tied observations expose the set of maximizers; an implementation's first index must not imply uniqueness. **Impact:** Exact Bayes risk is independent of which tied maximizer is selected under the uniform model, so no simulation or tie-breaking theory is needed. Full code design and capacity stay omitted.
