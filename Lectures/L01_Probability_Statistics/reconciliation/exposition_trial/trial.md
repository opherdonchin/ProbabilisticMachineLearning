# Mathematical exposition trial — L01 opening

Status: complete; negative result for the proposed discriminator. The instructor accepted the
coordinator's proposal with “That sounds useful. Why don't you try it.” No new
skill, permanent review stage, live-deck revision or whole-recording rewrite is
authorized by this trial. Current canonical decks and accepted notebook remain
the inputs. No candidate was built: the first-read reviewer found no material
exposition issue and proposed no conceptual repair, meeting the agreed stopping
condition rather than supplying findings on which to base a revision.

## Question and comparison

Can a fresh reader identify concrete mathematical-exposition gaps missed by
the previous review, distinguish them from legitimate abbreviation or preference,
and give findings that help improve one short recording sequence?

The two cases are the current recording opening through its first covariance
row operation and the accepted squared-loss sequence with its actual preceding
slide. Acceptance/status and instructor criticism are withheld from the fresh
reviewer. These are different mathematical tasks and excerpt lengths, not a
matched experiment or a test of student learning. An accepted comparator can
still receive valid criticism; its acceptance is not a requirement that a
reviewer find no faults.

## Exposure and ownership

The fresh `/root/fresh_exposition_read` agent started with no inherited chat.

The coordinator owns packaging, assessment, the trial's persistent evidence and

The first review is assessed against that pre-recorded basis, without requiring

Any later revision is a proposed mathematical opening, not accepted content or
proof that the review caused improvement. Its report must distinguish changes

accepted loss/notebook content remain intact.

## Result

The fresh reviewer endorsed both A (the accepted squared-loss sequence) and B
(the recording opening criticized by the instructor), with high confidence and
no material inferential gap or conceptual repair indicated. It recognized the
loss argument's centering, parallel moment calculations and term-attached
optimization reasoning. It also accepted the recording's forward-looking
proof commentary and covariance-cancellation transition, citing authentic
earlier residual/Schur/quadratic material as sufficient support.

The frozen [raw report](first_read/report.md) and [exposure record](first_read/exposure.md)
were preserved byte-for-byte before any further context. The reviewer saw every
target handout/reveal image at full supplied size and all selected prior-context
images. It did not read this discussion, source/design documents, prior reports,
skills or acceptance labels. No post-freeze prompt was used to seek agreement
with the instructor. [The receipt](receipt.json) retains the frozen hashes and
verifies canonical inputs were unchanged. The neutral PDF/text inputs remain
under `inputs/`; their PNG identities are in `coordinator_mapping.json` and can
be regenerated from those PDFs.

Against the pre-recorded concern list, the reviewer did not flag the dimension
subscript on the Gaussian or the unexplained positive-definiteness symbol; it
accepted absent explicit mean/covariance recall and the density-coefficient
framing. It treated the covariance transition as supported by prior lecture
knowledge. This is a fair record of its judgment, not proof that the slides
meet the instructor's exposition standard. It did not discriminate the known
concerns in the way the proposed additional review was intended to do.

## Coordinator assessment and next action

Do not add a review stage or new skill on this evidence. The trial weakens the
specific explanation that shared design history was the main cause of the
earlier favorable judgments: a fresh, artifact-first reviewer also endorsed the
recording opening. The report's argument reconstruction shows a permissive
standard based on an expert's ability to recover the mathematics from current
and prior material. It provides no demonstrated improvement in the clarity or
teachability that the instructor is seeking.

The bounded trial therefore stops with its negative result. No reviewer-based
repair, canonical deck change, workflow patch or further reviewer was commissioned.
Any next design work should use the instructor's concrete mathematical comments
and a small visible comparison for instructor judgment; it must not be presented
as a successful consequence of this review. This trial is one local test, not a
general finding that all independent exposition review is ineffective.
