## Mathematical and interpretive challenges

**Joint objective and gradients are constructible.** For each observation, q_phi is an encoder approximation to the current decoder model's posterior; it is not part of the generative factorization. A per-case KL belongs inside the observation average, unlike L09's single global-weight KL. At fixed tau²=1/2, reconstruction NLL is event-summed squared error plus `(19/2)log pi`; latent KL sums four coordinates. The pathwise sample uses `exp(ell/2)` because ell is log variance, not L09's log-SD rho. Reconstruction updates theta directly and phi through both encoder heads; exact KL updates phi. Fitted network weights remain points while z is a random per-case latent. Joint noisy training is not exact EM monotonicity or proof of MLE. These distinctions are explicit enough to be taught through bounded applications of prior results.

**Normalization and exact examples pass.** Independently recomputed the one-row example: KL=0.5, squared reconstruction residual=5, NLL `15.8749339155693`, beta-one loss `16.3749339155693`, beta-four loss `17.8749339155693`. The reconstruction value concerns one realized latent draw, not its expectation. Source loss is `S/19 + beta_source*r/4`; multiplying by 19 gives `S + (19 beta_source/4)r`. Thus source beta=4 corresponds to course beta=19, up to the fixed Normal constant. This is objective-scale equivalence only: multiplying loss can change an optimizer's dynamics, and changing preprocessing/model changes the fit. Source checkpoints cannot be relabeled course beta=4 or used as verified course fits.

**Rate proof is genuine new mathematics and appropriately bounded.** In the empirical joint `p_D(x)q_phi(z|x)`, insert the aggregate density into `log(q_phi(z|x)/p(z))`. The first expected log ratio is MI; marginalizing x makes the second aggregate KL. Nonnegative aggregate KL gives R≥MI without taking differential entropy of empirical x or claiming Shannon-optimal compression. The shifted-Normal oracle independently checks `I=0`, aggregate KL=R=0.5. It exposes prior mismatch rather than requires a numerical mixture-MI estimator. The 23-minute allocation is credible for definitions, this proof, one oracle and interpretation if broader IB/InfoVAE derivations remain omitted.

**Collapse and scientific interpretation are limited correctly.** Decoder independence from z implies model posterior=prior; q=prior for every x gives R=I=0. This is a valid ideal case, not a promise that the small fixed-variance decoder can model arbitrary data while ignoring z. Low fitted KL does not by itself demonstrate decoder insensitivity or successful fitting. Aggregate-prior mismatch and input information are distinct terms; reducing the former alone is not the stated collapse criterion. Correlations/traversals describe a fitted coordinate system, not causal factors, identified disentanglement or support-valid synthetic MSAs. Optional sign-flip equivalence preserves the prior/diagonal family and objective but is not a full nonidentifiability theorem.
