## Mathematical and computational checks with distinct implications

1. **A gradient check answers a software/correctness question.** L05's vector residual gradient, L09's complete sample derivative and L11/L12 scale/log-variance gradients must agree with the intended objective. That does not show an optimizer converges or that a student understands the objective. Shape checks must exclude silent cross-case broadcasting; objective normalization must distinguish a sum over observations/features/latent coordinates from a batch mean.
2. **Exactness under a model and suitability for a task differ.** HMM/Kalman recurrences can agree with enumeration or Gaussian conditioning to numerical precision. Their statistical interpretation still depends on the supplied transitions/emissions/dynamics matching the sequence task. Synthetic truth can test error explicitly but does not establish experience with noisy scientific data.
3. **Improving a fitting objective need not improve every prediction.** Gaussian EM's touching-bound argument relies on exact conditional sufficient statistics, including covariance. Observed training likelihood and held-out reconstruction quality answer different questions. A mean-imputed baseline or one fortunate complete-case fit can have smaller empirical reconstruction error without contradicting EM's guarantee.
4. **A common prediction task enables comparison without requiring a winner.** Laplace mode/curvature, VI's family/criterion and finite MH estimates are different objects. A small proper-prior logistic model is log-concave and may give very similar held-out scores. Keep an exact Gaussian dependence diagnostic and multimodal sampler stress test, rather than choosing data to force dramatic rankings. Computation time on a tiny case establishes local feasibility, not a realistic scaling advantage.
5. **A fast CPU fit is useful evidence, not a measured student hour.** Runtime can remove a computational obstacle. First PyTorch exposure, data/model roles, gradient/shape interpretation, and several distinct evaluation objects can still dominate student effort. The three knowledge categories and a supplied-versus-student action inventory are needed alongside seconds/iterations.
6. **VAE outputs require the correct source distribution and units.** Expected-q reconstruction, decoding the encoder mean, decoding a prior latent draw and sampling decoder observation noise are different operations. The rate is average encoder-to-prior KL, not automatically MI. Training-only transformations and consistently normalized beta runs matter more than a visually attractive latent plot. Domain validity after inverse transformation must be checked separately from tensor correctness.
