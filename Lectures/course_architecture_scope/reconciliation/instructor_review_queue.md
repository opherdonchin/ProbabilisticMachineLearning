# Instructor review queue

This is a compact index of unresolved questions, not another authority for teaching decisions. Each linked owning artifact contains the question, options, recommendation, evidence and affected work. Accepted answers are recorded in the owning lecture scope; this index then records resolution by link.

**Operating instruction, instructor 2026-10-14:** investigate in parallel with deferred review. Record instructor-owned choices without resolving them, wait only the affected tasks, continue other independent work and examine conditional alternatives where useful. Present the highest-leverage unresolved question **only when the instructor asks for review**, one at a time. Silence is not approval. A pending item does not block the investigation as a whole.
