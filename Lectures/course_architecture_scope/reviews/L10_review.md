## Reachability and source fidelity

The complete five-part scope has a coherent dependency path: IID estimator error, previously untaught invariance and convergence distinctions, a general MH correctness argument, bounded Gibbs cancellation, then dependent-query error and the proposal-comparison lab. No new theorem is borrowed from undergraduate sampler use. Protected L02 `section_design.md` explicitly stops before §2.6.4; its transition-count learning supplies notation, not stationarity. L03 supplies full-conditional cancellation and deterministic ICM; L07 supplies query/reference discipline. L09's Gaussian/pathwise work is useful context but not an MH prerequisite. An HMM transition model is correctly distinguished from an inference sampler.

Actual B2 §§2.6.4, 11.2, 12.2, 12.3 and 12.6 support the selected mathematics. The scope improves on potentially misleading source shorthand: invariance does not imply mixing, periodicity does not destroy a finite irreducible chain's invariant distribution, correlated samples need not inflate variance, and a finite discard does not establish stationarity. The stationary finite-N covariance formula includes the correct `(1−k/N)` edge factor. Stationarity/finite variance, summability and additional CLT conditions are separately named; query-specific ESS and failed-exploration caveats are preserved.

[Geyer's primary notes](https://www.stat.umn.edu/geyer/f05/8931/n1995.pdf), printed pp. 57–58, support Harris recurrence for an irreducible chain iterating one MH elementary update and integrable-average LLNs; aperiodic Harris convergence is stated on printed p. 62. This supports the scope's full-dimensional MH application, not an unrestricted assertion about arbitrary composed coordinate samplers. [Geyer's geometric-ergodicity notes](https://www.stat.umn.edu/geyer/8054/notes/geometric.html), §3, support the stated sufficient CLT example of geometric ergodicity and a `2+δ` moment. The scope does not claim to have established geometric ergodicity for its lab or that summable covariances alone imply a CLT.

MH uses the full forward/reverse proposal ratio, cancellation of Z, the symmetric minimum-flux proof and retained rejection states. These are enough to establish invariance without conflating proposal q with transition T. For the positive proper mixture and Gaussian random walk, accepted transitions have positive density across the support; rejections supply stays. Gibbs acceptance-one cancellation is correct. Coordinate kernels are reversible, their systematic composition preserves the joint but need not be reversible, and fixed-probability random-scan mixtures are reversible. HMC's `p~N(0,M)` with kinetic energy `½pᵀM⁻¹p` is consistent despite the inspected book's inconsistent covariance/inverse-mass expressions. HMC remains recognition only; no extra implementation/proof or routine NUTS-diagnostic tutorial is promised.
