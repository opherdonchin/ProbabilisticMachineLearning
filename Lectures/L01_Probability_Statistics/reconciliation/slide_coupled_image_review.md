## Reconstructed visible argument

Actual63 distinguishes observing y, conditioning to the Gaussian posterior p(z|y), and extracting the mean estimate z-hat = E[z|y] = mu_(z|y). Its two states show that summarizing follows conditioning and give E[z|y] as the posterior integral. It establishes that the mean is one summary, but does not yet justify choosing it as a reconstruction action.

Candidate64 carries precisely that mean estimate forward. It fixes an observed y, an unknown vector z in R^d, and an action a in R^d fixed at this y; assumes E[||z||²|y] < infinity; and defines squared loss L(a,z)=||z-a||² and conditional risk R(a|y)=E[L(a,z)|y]. The first key point explains choosing a by minimizing this posterior expected squared error. The second state defines m=E[z|y], V=Cov[z|y], and r=z-m under the local instruction “Center at m”; it maps m and V to the earlier Gaussian quantities. The third state writes z-a=r+(m-a) and expands the whole pointwise squared norm. Its second key point identifies the random residual and fixed offset. This is a meaningful preparation of the risk argument, not a collection of disconnected formulas.

Candidate65 recalls the prior definitions and pointwise expansion without falsely labeling the newly averaged identity as recalled knowledge. State 2 instructs taking conditional expectation with a,m fixed at this y and displays the full three-term risk expansion. State 3 supplies both load-bearing reasons: E[r|y]=E[z|y]-m=0, hence E[r^T(m-a)|y]=E[r|y]^T(m-a)=0; and V=E[rr^T|y], hence E[||r||²|y]=sum_j E[r_j²|y]=sum_j V_jj=tr V. State 4 visibly substitutes those moments into the intact identity R(a|y)=tr V+||m-a||², accompanied by the interpretation of residual uncertainty plus squared displacement from the mean. State 5 uses nonnegativity with equality iff a=m to obtain the unique action a*(y)=m=E[z|y] and minimum conditional risk tr V, then maps both the action and minimum back to the earlier Gaussian mean and covariance. The second key point identifies model expected loss and explicitly says Gaussianity is unnecessary when conditional second moments are finite.

Actual66 then applies the same conditioning-to-mean operations to two noisy measurements of one state, with a simultaneous three-case uncertainty schematic and cumulative takeaways. The insertion has answered why the posterior mean is selected before that mean is used to interpret sensor fusion.

## Weaknesses and teaching consequences

No material mathematical omission or erroneous inference was found in the images. I judge the insertion feasible as a visible argument, with a presentation-density risk rather than a mathematical blocker.

The risk is concentrated in candidate65's completed state: the recall, risk expansion, two left-side justifications, right-side identity and optimizer, and two wrapping key points fill most of the usable vertical field. State 3 adds both moment justifications at once.
